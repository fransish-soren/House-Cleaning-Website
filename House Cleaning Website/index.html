A few good questions

Wondering about something?

Here are a few helpful details before your first SparkNest clean.

Still have a question? Get in touch →
What’s included in a standard cleaning?+

Standard cleaning generally covers surfaces, dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom touch-ups, and tidying common areas. Exact tasks can be confirmed when your visit is arranged.

How is the estimate calculated?+

Your estimate is based on service type, bedroom and bathroom count, visit frequency, and any selected add-ons. The calculator is illustrative; final pricing may depend on your home and service details.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?+

That depends on your local team and booking arrangement. We’ll confirm what’s provided and discuss any product preferences before the appointment.

Can I reschedule my appointment?+

We aim to be flexible. Contact the team as early as possible to ask about rescheduling and any applicable booking policy.

Are recurring visits discounted?+

Yes. This sample estimate applies 10% off for every-two-week visits and 15% off for weekly visits, before add-ons.

How do I prepare for the cleaner’s arrival?+

Put away valuables, secure pets if needed, and let us know about access, parking, or delicate items. No need to pre-clean—we’re here to help.
